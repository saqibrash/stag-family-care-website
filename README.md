# STAG Family Care Website

Create a complete, polished, responsive website for a new UK care business called STAG Family Care.

This is a separate new business connected to an existing local company called STAG Cleaning Services. Existing website for brand context: https://www.stagcleaningservices.co.uk/

The new care business should have its own identity, website and marketing, while keeping the STAG name and family reputation. The name STAG comes from family names, so family values are central to the brand.

Brand details:
Business name: STAG Family Care
Strapline: From our family to yours
Primary colour: bright pink #ff66c4
Use white, warm cream, soft blush, muted burgundy and dark charcoal as supporting colours.
The logo will likely use a stag as the main symbol. Use a tasteful temporary stag mark or elegant stag silhouette placeholder in the design until the final logo is supplied.

Business services:
Personal care
24/7 live-in care
Companionship
Supported living

Important: Do not claim that the business is CQC registered, regulated, approved, award-winning, or already established unless this is later confirmed. Add a clearly editable note or placeholder where registration details may be added later. Avoid unrealistic promises about care outcomes.

Style and visual direction:
Professional UK care-sector website
Warm, caring, calm, trustworthy and family-focused
Modern premium look without feeling clinical or corporate
Clear typography and excellent readability
Not too much content and not too little
Use natural British English
Generous spacing and simple navigation
Fully responsive and accessible
Strong contrast and keyboard-friendly interactions

Create subtle high-quality 3D visual elements and motion, but keep performance and trust in mind. Do not make the whole website feel like a gaming site. Use tasteful 3D sections such as:
A floating layered stag emblem or soft 3D family/home composition in the hero
Subtle depth, glass layers and soft parallax
Interactive 3D service cards with gentle tilt on desktop
A small animated pathway showing enquiry, assessment, matching and ongoing support
Respect prefers-reduced-motion and provide static fallbacks on mobile or low-power devices
No heavy video background

Build a multi-page website or a well-structured routed website with these pages:
Home
About Us
Our Services
Personal Care
Live-in Care
Companionship
Supported Living
Why Choose Us
Work With Us / Careers
Contact
Privacy Policy placeholder

Home page sections:
1. Top announcement strip with an editable launch message
2. Header with logo placeholder, navigation and clear Enquire About Care button
3. Hero with the heading: Care that feels personal, because family comes first.
Supporting copy explaining that STAG Family Care provides respectful, dependable support designed around each person and their independence.
Buttons: Enquire About Care and Explore Our Services
4. Trust/value highlights: Dignity, Independence, Consistency, Family values
5. Services section with four clear cards
6. Why families choose STAG section
7. How support begins in four simple steps: Tell us what you need, Initial conversation, Personal care plan, Ongoing support
8. Family story section explaining the meaning behind STAG and the connection to STAG Cleaning Services without confusing the two businesses
9. Local care section with editable service-area placeholders
10. Testimonials section clearly labelled as placeholder content to replace before launch. Do not invent real names or reviews.
11. FAQ section
12. Strong contact CTA
13. Detailed footer

About page:
Explain the family-led idea, values and purpose. Include editable placeholders for the founders' names, care experience, qualifications, service area, registration status and launch date.

Service pages:
Each service page should explain who the service may suit, what support can include, how the process works, and a clear enquiry CTA. Use careful wording such as 'may include' and 'subject to assessment'.

Careers page:
Warm recruitment page for carers and support workers with placeholders for roles, requirements, benefits and application email. Do not imply current vacancies unless marked as editable.

Contact page:
Create an enquiry form with fields for name, phone, email, postcode, type of support, preferred contact method, message, consent checkbox and privacy note. Include editable placeholders for phone, email, office address, opening hours and emergency/out-of-hours guidance. The form can work as a front-end demo and should have clear success/error states.

SEO and technical requirements:
Use semantic HTML
Unique page titles and meta descriptions
LocalBusiness/HealthAndBeautyBusiness-style structured data only where appropriate, without false regulatory claims
Open Graph tags
Good heading structure
Optimised loading
Accessible alt text
Sitemap and robots setup if supported
Use clean URLs
Add editable location placeholders for future local SEO pages

Typography:
Use a refined serif for large headings paired with a highly readable modern sans-serif for body copy. Make body text comfortable for older users and families.

Important final details:
Add a small floating contact button on mobile, but keep it unobtrusive.
Use icons thoughtfully, not everywhere.
Do not use fake statistics.
Do not use fake accreditations.
Do not use fake testimonials.
Do not use medical imagery that feels distressing.
Create the complete website in TypeScript with Tailwind and polished reusable components.
Use temporary placeholders for phone number, email, address, final logo, team names, service areas and regulatory details, and make them easy to find and update.

Please build the full first version now, with refined visual design, working navigation, responsive layouts, tasteful 3D motion, and realistic website copy ready for client review.
